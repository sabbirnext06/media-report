Q3 Audience & Retention Strategy

CFDs Audience Strategy

  • Heavier focus on Consideration & Conversion Funnel — retaining existing customers and winning back churn audiences
  • Awareness: Reach & Frequency on Meta Ads for high ad frequency
  • YouTube as top priority for Awareness and Custom Audience Retargeting
  • Strategy detailed on Miro board for cross-team alignment

Futures Audience Strategy

  • Acquire: Competitor, interest, keyword, lookalike & YouTube audiences across Google, Meta, Bing — amplified by Twitter, TikTok & DV360
  • Retarget: 180D engagers (checkout push), 30D high-intent (purchase push), lifetime buyers via PMax
  • Convert: Brand Keywords, Google Predictive Audience, Bing Signal, Meta 365D Engagers, Twitter retargeting — all optimized to Purchase Futures

Offer Strategy — Full Funnel Approach

Google

YouTube Video Views + Demand Gen for visibility. PMax as core conversion engine with 90D Events & Predictive Audience (Bid for New Only). Search captures high-intent demand.

Meta

Reach campaigns for offer exposure. Sales campaigns re-engage website visitors and convert high-intent audiences.

Bing

Extend existing Search for active demand. Dedicated PMax campaigns targeting incremental conversions.

TikTok

Interest & hashtag-based Traffic campaigns. Spark Ads retargeting to convert engaged website visitors.

Churn Audience Recovery

Objective
Re-engage H2 2025 purchasers with no H1 2026 activity
Target
At least 1 purchase Jul–Dec 2025, zero in Jan–Jun 2026. Segmented by plan size, purchase value, and region.
Channels
Google, Meta, TikTok & all retargeting-enabled platforms
Offer
Incentive-based — discount, bonus, or limited-time deal (to be finalized)
Messaging
Urgency-driven 7-day window. Single-purchase churners get feature-highlight creatives ("We've improved"). Multi-purchase churners get loyalty-framed messaging ("We miss you + reward").
